# P14 — Power BI Interactive Report

Design a three-page Power BI report.

Page 1 Academic Performance Overview: Total Students, Average Marks, Pass Percentage, performance distribution, pass/fail and department results.

Page 2 Student and Subject Analysis: Top 10, rankings, subject average and pass percentage, student drill-through.

Page 3 Attendance and Academic Risk: average attendance, low-attendance list, attendance versus marks and subject attendance.

For every visual specify type, fields, DAX, filters, slicers, drill-through and formatting. Give click-by-click Desktop instructions and refresh/validation steps. A PBIX is not complete until actually created and saved.

**Output:** `powerbi/Student_Performance_Dashboard.pbix` and `docs/PowerBI_Report_Guide.md`.
